Automatic Screw Fastening Station

Why this product

What it gives you

Traceable torque

Every screw is driven to a programmed torque window and the result is logged against the serial number.

Hands-free feeding

An automatic feeder presents one screw at a time, removing pick-up errors and dropped fasteners.

Mistake proofing

Wrong sequence, cross-threading or a stripped thread stops the cycle and raises an alarm instead of passing silently.

What it does

The operator loads the assembly, starts the cycle and the station steps through each fastening point in sequence. A servo-driven spindle reaches target torque, captures the peak value and reports pass or fail immediately.

Fixture plates are swappable, so one station can serve several products. Because the sequence and torque values are recipes rather than hard-wired logic, new variants are added without rewiring.

Applications

  • Control panels and enclosures with many fasteners
  • Sub-assemblies where torque is a quality characteristic
  • Lines that must keep a per-unit fastening record
  • Manual cells that need consistent output across shifts

What we hand over

  • Tool head and matching feeder for your screw type
  • Swappable fixture plate for one product variant
  • Torque report export over Ethernet or USB
  • Operator training and a torque verification gauge
