Why this product

What it gives you

One analyser, many test types

Peak, breakaway, drag and travel tests share the same instrument and the same reporting format.

Traceable measurements

Load cells and torque transducers are supplied with calibration data so results survive an audit.

Curves, not just numbers

Force-displacement and torque-angle curves make intermittent faults visible instead of hiding behind a single peak value.

What it does

A motorized test stand moves the sample while the instrument samples the transducer at high rate. Limits are defined per test, and every run produces a curve plus a pass/fail decision that can be exported for a quality record.

Because the stand speed and travel are programmable, the same test can be repeated exactly by different operators — which is usually the difference between a useful measurement and an argument.

Applications

  • Switch, push-button and connector feel testing
  • Fastener breakaway and strip torque studies
  • Actuator and damper characterization
  • Incoming inspection of purchased mechanisms

What we hand over

  • Instrument console with curve display
  • One force or torque transducer with calibration data
  • Universal mounting stand and grip set
  • Report templates and CSV export utility
