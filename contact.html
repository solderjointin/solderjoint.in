How to get a useful answer faster

  • Describe the process or product involved, and what currently goes wrong or takes too long.
  • Include a photo of the existing setup, part or panel if you have one.
  • State the target cycle time, accuracy or measurement range you need.
  • Mention the quantity: one-off, pilot batch or production volume.
  • Tell us what it must connect to — PLC, MES, cloud or nothing at all.

What happens next

  1. We read it properly You get a reply from someone who understands the application, not an automated acknowledgement.
  2. We ask the gaps Usually two or three questions about constraints, interfaces or volume.
  3. We tell you what is feasible Including when the honest answer is that you do not need what you asked for.
  4. We quote in writing Approach, scope, price and lead time in a document you can circulate internally.
