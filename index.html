How we work

From a conversation to a commissioned system

  1. Understand the requirementA conversation about the process, the target cycle time and the constraints you already have. No specification document needed to start.
  2. Design and quoteA concrete proposal with the approach, the interfaces, the price and the delivery expectation — written down so it can be reviewed.
  3. Build and proveMechanical, electrical and software work under one owner, followed by a factory acceptance run with your parts or panels.
  4. Commission and hand overInstallation at your site, operator training and documentation you can maintain after we leave.

Why customers keep us on their vendor list

  • One owner from concept to commissioning. Mechanical design, electrical design, software, build and site commissioning sit with the same team, so nothing falls between two suppliers.
  • Proven before it ships. Machines and fixtures run a factory acceptance routine with your parts, and you are invited to witness it.
  • Documented, open interfaces. PLC, Modbus, OPC-UA and MQTT integrations are documented, so your team is never locked out of its own equipment.
  • Support that understands the plant. Remote diagnostics, locally available spares and engineers who have stood on a shop floor at 2 a.m.
